Analytics for music

Streams are in one app, ticket sales in another, merch in a notebook and the money in a group chat. We bring them into one dashboard that answers your questions.

Questions a dashboard can answer

We start from what you want to know, not from the data you happen to have.

  • Which cities should we play next?
  • Which songs keep growing after a show?
  • What does a gig really earn once costs are paid?
  • Which merch sells, and in which sizes?
  • When does a release pay back what it cost?

An example

Three of the questions above, answered for one band's year. One answer per screen, the way we build them.

How it works

  1. Find the numbers

    We look at where your numbers live today: platforms, ticketing, spreadsheets, receipts.

  2. Pick the questions

    Together we choose the few questions that would change a decision.

  3. Build the dashboard

    One screen per question, readable on a phone, with every number traceable to its source.

  4. Keep it fresh

    Sources refresh on their own, so the dashboard is current when you open it.
