What we make

A studio in the way a game studio is one: we design, build and run software. Music is where we started, and where most of our work lives.

  • Apps

    For the way a band actually works: rehearsals, who can make it, setlists, money and merch. Built for the phone first, and quick on a weak signal.

    Example: Bandeon

  • Websites

    For bands and artists, and for shops, venues, studios and small businesses outside music: a site that loads fast, looks like it means it, and that search engines can read.

    Example: Tilt Guild

  • Design

    Logos and identities, album and single covers, posters for live shows, and the look of websites and apps. Drawn for the screen first, ready for print.

    Examples

  • Analytics

    Dashboards built on your own numbers: streams, shows, merch and money in one place, answering the questions you actually ask.

How we work

  1. Talk

    You tell us what gets in the way: the group chat, the spreadsheet, the gig nobody confirmed.

  2. Sketch

    We come back with a plan and a first screen you can try on your own phone.

  3. Build

    In small steps, each one working, each one tried by you before the next.

  4. Keep it running

    After launch we look after it: fast, secure and up to date.
