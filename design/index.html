Bandeon

An arched hall with five columns: the room where a band rehearses. Drawn on a grid, then corrected by eye.

  1. Crown The top of the arch sits on the grid.
  2. Spring line Every arch starts here.
  3. Oculus Centred by eye between the crown and the arches, not by the numbers.
  4. Entasis The slots open a little toward the top, so the columns taper like real ones and do not look heavy at the base.
  5. One light The ember dot is the only colour, and it lives only in the logo.
