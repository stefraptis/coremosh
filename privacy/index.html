Privacy and credits

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites while you read it.

The contact form

When you send a message, your name, email and message go to us through Formspree, a form service. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them once the conversation is over. You can ask us to delete them sooner through the same form.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may record technical details of each visit, such as the IP address, for security. We do not see or use them.
