Tilt Guild

The website of Tilt Guild, a rap metal band from Athens. Releases, shows, merch and press, in a look taken from their live shows.

Type
Website
Year
2026
Languages
English, bios in Greek
Live at
tiltguild.com

The problem

The band needed one address for every bio and every poster: a site that looks like their shows and gives fans, promoters and press what they came for, without anyone having to ask.

What we built

  • A look from the stage

    Full-screen black-and-white live photos, matched in tone so the slideshow never jumps, with one dark red accent and heavy condensed type.

  • Releases and shows

    A page per release with a player that loads only when pressed. Every show with tickets, an add-to-calendar file, and the details search engines need to list it as an event.

  • Merch and giveaways

    An order request form for shirts and CDs. Giveaways with a countdown, entries that land in a sheet the band shares, and a random winner picked in one click.

  • Press kit and links

    Bios in English and Greek, photos and logos to download, and a links page made for Instagram and TikTok bios.

How it is built

A static site with no framework and no cookies: one stylesheet, one small script, self-hosted fonts, and photos sized for every screen so a phone only loads what it shows.

Checked with an automated accessibility test at phone and desktop width, with no errors on any page.
