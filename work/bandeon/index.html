Bandeon

A workspace for bands and solo artists. The rehearsal calendar, when everyone is free, the songs and setlists, and the band's money, in one app on every member's phone.

Type
Web app, installs from the browser
Year
2026
Languages
Greek and English
Live at
bandeon.app
Screen loop: availability, setlist, stage mode

The problem

Bands run on group chats. Dates get lost between messages, the setlist lives in someone's notes app, and nobody remembers who paid for the rehearsal room.

What we built

  • Availability

    Each member adds the few hours they are free. The band sees the best days and times, who can make it and who can't, and books the rehearsal in one tap.

  • Calendar and reminders

    Rehearsals and gigs on any dates or on a repeat, answers from every member, and a reminder the day before. Members in several bands see when two of them clash.

  • Songs and stage mode

    Chords and lyrics pasted from anywhere, transposed with a tap. Stage mode shows one song at a time in large type, swiped from song to song, with the screen kept on.

  • Money

    Expenses, gig fees and the band's fund, split equally, by exact amounts or as a gift, with the fewest payments needed to settle up.

  • Merch

    Stock per size, sales recorded at the merch table, and every sale straight into the band's books.

How it is built

A progressive web app: it installs from the browser on iPhone and Android, with no app store in between. Greek first, English second, every text translatable from day one.

Built to be quick in a basement with one bar of signal. On a mid-range phone over 4G every main screen shows its content in under 1.5 seconds. The data stays in the EU, and every table is locked to the band it belongs to.
