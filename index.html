About the studio

We build and run software for the music industry, creating apps and websites for bands, artists and independent labels, alongside digital solutions for creative projects.

Coremosh brings together two things we know firsthand: music and software. As musicians ourselves, we understand the challenges of running a band and the tools that can make things easier. We develop software shaped by real needs, informed by our own experience and refined through everyday use.

Bandeon

A dedicated workspace for bands and solo artists to organise rehearsals, coordinate schedules, manage their music and keep track of finances. Everything in one place, accessible to every member from their own phone.

Visit bandeon.app
  • Availability and scheduling Members share their availability, making it easy to find suitable dates and organise rehearsals around everyone's schedule.
  • Calendar and events Keep rehearsals and gigs organised in one shared calendar, track attendance and receive reminders ahead of upcoming events.
  • Songs and stage mode Keep lyrics, chords and song arrangements organised in one place. Transpose songs instantly and access everything through a dedicated, distraction-free stage interface.
  • Finances and merchandise Track shared expenses, gig income, band funds and merchandise sales. Keep everyone's contributions and balances clear, with simplified expense settlements.

Tilt Guild

The official website of an Athens-based rap metal band, designed as a central hub for fans, promoters and press. Its visual identity draws inspiration from the band's music, live performances and uncompromising aesthetic.

Visit tiltguild.com
  • Visual identity Full-screen live photography, a dark colour palette with restrained red accents, and bold condensed typography inspired by the band's stage presence.
  • Releases and live shows Dedicated pages for music releases and upcoming shows, featuring event details, ticket information and calendar integration.
  • Merchandise and giveaways A streamlined merchandise request system for shirts and CDs, alongside interactive giveaways with countdowns and automated winner selection.
  • Press kit and social links A dedicated press area featuring English and Greek band biographies, downloadable promotional photography and logos, plus a centralised page for social media and streaming links.

How we work

From the first conversation to long after launch.

  1. Discover

    We start by understanding your goals, requirements and the challenges you want to address. Together, we define what needs to be built and how it should work.

  2. Design

    We translate your ideas into a clear plan and an initial design, giving you an early opportunity to explore and provide feedback on the proposed solution.

  3. Develop

    We build in manageable stages, testing each component and sharing progress with you throughout the development process.

  4. Maintain

    Our work continues beyond launch. We provide ongoing maintenance, updates and improvements to keep your software reliable and up to date.
