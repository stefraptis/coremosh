About the studio

Coremosh software studios

We design, build and run software: apps and websites for bands, artists and labels, design to go with them, and websites for anyone else.

We play in bands, and we build software for a living. Coremosh is where the two meet. We make the tools we wished our own bands had, and we use them every week. That is how we find out what is missing.

How we work

  1. Talk

    You tell us what gets in the way: the group chat, the spreadsheet, the gig nobody confirmed.

  2. Sketch

    We come back with a plan and a first screen you can try on your own phone.

  3. Build

    In small steps, each one working, each one tried by you before the next.

  4. Keep it running

    After launch we look after it and keep it up to date.
