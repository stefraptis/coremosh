A software studio with music at its core

Not a recording studio. A studio in the way a game studio is one: we design, build and run our own software, and build for others. Apps and analytics for music, websites for anyone.

Who we are

We play in bands, and we build software for a living: apps, websites and the analytics behind business decisions. Coremosh is where the two meet.

We make the tools we wished our own bands had, and we use them every week. That is how we find out what is missing.

What we hold to

  • The phone comes first

    Musicians check things in vans, basements and backstage. Everything we make works one-handed on a phone.

  • Fast on a bad signal

    We set a speed budget for every screen and treat a missed budget as a bug.

  • Your data stays yours

    No selling, no tracking for its own sake, data kept in the EU, and a way to download or delete it.

  • Finished over big

    A small thing that works this week beats a large one that might work next year.

Working on something?

We are glad to hear about it, whether it is an app, a website or a pile of numbers.
